# Intrinsic 2D-XY Ferromagnetism in a van der Waals Monolayer (Bedoya-Pinto et al. 2021)

**Authors**: Amilcar Bedoya-Pinto, Jing-Rong Ji, Avanindra Pandeya, Pierluigi Gargiani, Manuel Valvidares, Paolo Sessi, Florin Radu, Kai Chang, Stuart S. P. Parkin  
**Journal**: Science 374, 616-620 (2021) / arXiv:2006.07605v2  
**DOI**: 10.1126/science.abd5146  

## Summary of Key Findings for Monolayer CrCl3

1. **True Monolayer Isolation & Structure**:
   - First scalable, atomically thin van der Waals CrCl3 monolayer grown by molecular-beam epitaxy (MBE) on graphene/6H-SiC(0001).
   - In-plane honeycomb Cr lattice coordinated octahedrally to Cl atoms with van der Waals gap to substrate.
   - Atom-resolved STM and RHEED confirm hexagonal monolayer lattice with characteristic moire patterns.

2. **Intrinsic 2D-XY Ferromagnetism**:
   - Monolayer CrCl3 exhibits strong in-plane magnetic anisotropy (easy-plane ferromagnet, 2D-XY universality class).
   - Finite-size Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless (BKT) topological phase transition with TBKT ~ 13 K.
   - Contrasts with CrI3 (which has strong out-of-plane Ising anisotropy due to strong SOC of heavy Iodine).

3. **Electronic & Structural Implications**:
   - Weak spin-orbit coupling in Cl ensures pristine easy-plane continuous rotational symmetry in the xy-plane.
   - Monolayer thickness d ~ 2.6 - 2.7 A, with Cr atoms centered vertically between top and bottom Cl sublayers.
