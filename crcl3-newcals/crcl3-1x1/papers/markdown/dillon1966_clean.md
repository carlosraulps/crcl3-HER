# Magneto-Optical Properties of Ferromagnetic Chromium Trihalides (Dillon et al. 1966)

**Authors**: J. F. Dillon, Jr., H. Kamimura, and J. P. Remeika  
**Journal**: Journal of Physics and Chemistry of Solids, Vol. 27, pp. 1531-1549 (1966)  
**DOI**: 10.1016/0022-3697(66)90148-X  

## Summary of Key Findings for CrCl3

1. **Crystal Structure & Lattice**:
   - CrCl3 crystallizes in the BiI3-type rhombohedral structure (space group R-3) at low temperature, with hexagonal equivalent in-plane lattice parameter a = 6.048 A.
   - Ferromagnetic honeycomb layers of Cr3+ octahedrally coordinated by Cl- ions, coupled weakly by van der Waals bonds along c.

2. **Optical Absorption Spectrum**:
   - Crystal-field intra-ionic 3d-3d transitions observed at 1.68 eV (4A2g -> 4T2g) and ~2.3 eV (4A2g -> 4T1g).
   - Sharp absorption lines (spin-forbidden transitions 4A2g -> 2Eg, 2T1g) in the region 1.6 - 1.7 eV.
   - Steep fundamental absorption edge above 3.0 - 3.2 eV attributed to interband charge-transfer transitions (Cl 3p -> Cr 3d).

3. **Magnetic Ordering**:
   - Ferromagnetic in-plane coupling within each monolayer layer.
   - Antiferromagnetic inter-layer coupling in bulk with Neel temperature TN = 16.8 K, and paramagnetic Curie-Weiss theta = +40 K.
